## 🎯 What You Will Understand After This Module

By taking your time through this module, you will master:
1. **File Extensions & Files:** Exactly what `.py`, `.md`, `.exe`, `.cfg`, and `.gitignore` mean and why computers care about them.
2. **The Terminal & The OS:** What PowerShell actually is, and the secret "Address Book" (`PATH`) Windows uses to find programs.
3. **The Interpreter:** The difference between Python the *language* and Python the *software program* (`python.exe`).
4. **Scoop & Packaging:** Why you have Scoop, what `pip` does, and why the global "App Store" approach creates messes.
5. **Virtual Environments (`.venv`):** The clean "Toolbox" mental model that solves dependency chaos forever.
6. **Git Boundaries:** Why Git only ever stores your recipes (code), never your pantry or kitchen equipment (`.venv`).
